NexaTech Incremental Ingestion Test Document 58

Document ID: TEST-058
Department: Engineering
Category: Incremental Indexing
Status: Active

This document tests the ability of the NexaTech retrieval
system to identify newly added documents, extract their
contents, generate token-aware chunks, create embeddings,
and store them in the ChromaDB vector database.

Each test document has a unique identifier and content.
Previously indexed documents must not be embedded again.
